## Exercise 3: Academic Integrity Policy Document

# Seneca Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

To maintain a high standard of academic integrity across the Seneca community and provide the foundation for research, teaching, learning and working practices.

## Key Definitions

### Academic Integrity

> Academic integrity is a commitment to the fundamental values of honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility and courage within an academic environment.

### Cheating

**Cheating** involves obtaining or attempting to obtain credit for work or improvement in performance evaluation through dishonest or deceptive means.

### Contract Cheating

**Contract cheating** occurs when a student submits academic work created by a third party as if it were their own work for academic credit.

### Falsification/Fabrication

**Falsification/fabrication** includes misrepresenting another student's academic work or creating, altering or reporting false or inaccurate information to gain an academic advantage.

### Impersonation

**Impersonation** includes taking a test, examination or another assessment on behalf of another individual.

### Plagiarism

**Plagiarism** involves using another person's or organization's work and presenting it as one's own without properly citing the source.

### Self-Plagiarism

**Self-plagiarism** is the unauthorized reuse of one's own work from previous assignments.

### Unauthorized Aid

**Unauthorized aid** means using non-approved support to complete coursework or assessments.

### Undeclared AI Use

**Undeclared AI use** means using artificial intelligence to generate part or all of an assessment without the required disclosure.

## Policy

### 1. General

- Seneca supports a learning community that values **academic integrity, honesty, fairness, trust, respect, responsibility and courage**.
- Seneca is committed to high-quality education and a positive, equitable and inclusive learning environment.

### 2. Academic Integrity Program

- Academic integrity helps ensure that academic credentials accurately represent students' knowledge and learning.
- Seneca's academic integrity program includes policy, procedures, development and training.

### 3. Responsibilities

- Seneca provides information and resources to help students understand academic integrity.
- Students are responsible for informing faculty about circumstances affecting their academic performance as soon as possible.
- Students who witness or suspect an academic integrity violation can report it according to the appropriate procedure.

### 4. Sanctions

- Academic integrity sanctions are applied according to the severity of the violation.
- Before final decisions or sanctions are applied, relevant circumstances and the student's record may be considered.

## Academic Integrity Procedure

| Action | Duration | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Report a witnessed or suspected academic integrity violation | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, student, support staff |
| Inform the student of the witnessed or suspected offence and discuss the situation | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| Collect and retain the relevant assessment and evidence | Within five days of the reported violation | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| If no offence occurred, inform the student | Within two Seneca business days | Faculty |
| If an offence occurred, inform the student and begin the formal process | Within five Seneca business days | Faculty |
